Space Discoveries

Discoveries That Changed Our Understanding of the Universe

🌌

Expanding Universe

Observations of distant galaxies showed that the universe is expanding. This discovery transformed our understanding of the large-scale history and evolution of the cosmos.

🕳️

Black Holes

Theoretical physics predicted black holes, and astronomical observations have since provided strong evidence for their existence and effects on surrounding matter and light.

📡

Cosmic Microwave Background

The cosmic microwave background is faint radiation left from the early universe. Its discovery provided important evidence for the hot, dense early state of the cosmos.

🪐

Exoplanets

Scientists have discovered thousands of planets orbiting stars outside our Solar System. These worlds have greatly expanded our knowledge of planetary systems.

💥

Gravitational Waves

The detection of gravitational waves opened a new way of observing the universe and provided direct evidence of powerful cosmic events such as merging black holes.

🔭

Deep Space Observations

Modern telescopes have allowed scientists to observe extremely distant galaxies and study stars and cosmic structures across vast periods of cosmic history.

🔬 How Do We Discover Space?

Astronomers use telescopes, spacecraft, detectors and mathematical models to investigate the universe. Different forms of light, particles and gravitational waves provide different windows into cosmic phenomena.

🌌 Galaxies

Astronomers observe galaxies across enormous cosmic distances.

🪐 Exoplanets

Thousands of planets have been confirmed beyond our Solar System.

📡 CMB

Ancient radiation provides information about the early universe.

〰️ Waves

Gravitational waves provide another way to study cosmic events.

🔭 Telescopes

Observatories collect information from across the electromagnetic spectrum.

🚀 Spacecraft

Robotic missions explore planets, moons and distant regions of space.
