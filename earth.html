Earth

Our Blue Planet — The Known World of Life

About Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the largest of the four terrestrial planets in our Solar System. It is the only world currently known to support life.

Earth has vast oceans, continents, mountains, deserts, forests and polar regions. Its combination of liquid water, a protective atmosphere and suitable temperatures makes it a remarkable planet.

Atmosphere

Earth's atmosphere is mainly composed of nitrogen and oxygen, along with smaller amounts of other gases. It protects life from harmful solar radiation and helps regulate the planet's temperature.

The atmosphere also creates weather and climate systems that continuously move energy and water around the planet.

The Water World

About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by water. Most of this water is found in oceans, while smaller amounts exist in glaciers, rivers, lakes, groundwater and the atmosphere.

Liquid water is one of the most important ingredients for life as we know it.

Life on Earth

Earth contains an extraordinary diversity of life, ranging from microscopic organisms to plants, animals and complex ecosystems.

Life exists in many different environments, including oceans, deserts, forests, mountains and even extreme environments where conditions are difficult for most organisms.

Earth and the Moon

Earth has one natural satellite: the Moon. The Moon is the fifth-largest natural satellite in the Solar System and plays an important role in Earth's tides.

The gravitational interaction between Earth and the Moon also influences the length of Earth's day over very long periods.

Rotation & Revolution

Earth rotates once approximately every 24 hours, producing the cycle of day and night.

Earth completes one orbit around the Sun in approximately 365.25 days. This extra fraction of a day is why leap years are used to keep our calendar aligned with Earth's orbit.

Exploring Our Planet

Earth has been studied from the ground, from aircraft and from thousands of satellites in space. These observations help scientists understand weather, climate, oceans, ecosystems and changes occurring across the planet.

Interesting Facts

  • Earth is the third planet from the Sun.
  • It is the only planet currently known to support life.
  • About 71% of its surface is covered by water.
  • Earth has one natural satellite, the Moon.
  • Its atmosphere is mainly nitrogen and oxygen.
  • Earth has active plate tectonics that reshape its surface.
