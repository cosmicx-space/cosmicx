About Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only world currently known to support life. Its liquid-water oceans, protective atmosphere and suitable temperatures make it an extraordinary home for millions of known species. Earth's surface is constantly changing through weather, oceans, volcanoes and moving tectonic plates.

☀️ Distance

About 149.6 million km from the Sun on average.

🌊 Surface

About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by water.

⏱️ Year

About 365.25 days to orbit the Sun.

🌡️ Atmosphere

Mostly nitrogen and oxygen, supporting life at the surface.

🌙 Natural Moon

Earth has one natural satellite: the Moon.

🔄 Rotation

One rotation takes about 24 hours.

Discover Earth

01 · Blue Oceans

Earth's oceans regulate climate, store heat and provide habitats for an enormous diversity of marine life.

02 · Protective Atmosphere

The atmosphere helps regulate temperatures and shields the surface from many small meteoroids and harmful solar radiation.

03 · Moving Continents

Earth's outer shell is divided into tectonic plates that move slowly, creating mountains, earthquakes and volcanoes.

04 · The Moon

The Moon influences ocean tides and helps stabilize Earth's axial tilt over long timescales.
