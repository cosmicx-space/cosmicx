Explore the Galaxy

Discover the amazing structures found inside galaxies.

🌟

Galactic Core

The central region containing a dense concentration of stars. The Milky Way's centre contains Sagittarius A*.

🌀

Spiral Arms

Curving regions filled with stars, gas and dust where new stars can form.

🌌

Nebulae

Vast clouds of gas and dust that can become stellar nurseries.

✨

Star Clusters

Groups of stars held together by gravity, ranging from young clusters to ancient globular clusters.

⚫

Black Holes

Regions where gravity is so strong that nothing, not even light, can escape from within the event horizon.

💫

Cosmic Dust

Tiny particles that absorb and scatter light, creating dark lanes throughout a galaxy.
