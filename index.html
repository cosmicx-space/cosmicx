Where Matter Becomes Energy

Nuclear fusion combines light atomic nuclei under extreme conditions. This is the process that powers the Sun and other stars.

Extreme Heat

Fusion plasma in experimental reactors can reach temperatures above 100 million degrees Celsius.

Magnetic Fields
