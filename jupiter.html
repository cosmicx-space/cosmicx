Jupiter

The Giant of the Solar System

About Jupiter

Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun and the largest planet in our Solar System. It is a gas giant composed mainly of hydrogen and helium.

Its enormous size and powerful gravity make Jupiter one of the most fascinating objects in the Solar System.

The Great Red Spot

Jupiter is famous for the Great Red Spot, a gigantic storm in its atmosphere. The storm has been observed for centuries and is larger than Earth.

Atmosphere

Jupiter's atmosphere contains colourful bands of clouds created by powerful winds and atmospheric chemistry. Storms and turbulent weather are common throughout the planet.

Moons

Jupiter has a large family of moons. The four largest are Io, Europa, Ganymede and Callisto, known collectively as the Galilean moons.

Exploring Jupiter

Spacecraft have studied Jupiter's atmosphere, magnetic field, rings and moons. Robotic missions continue to improve our understanding of this enormous gas giant.

Interesting Facts

  • Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System.
  • It is a gas giant.
  • It has a very powerful magnetic field.
  • The Great Red Spot is a giant atmospheric storm.
  • Jupiter has a faint ring system.
  • Its rotation is extremely fast for a planet of its size.
