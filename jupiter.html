King of the Planets

JUPITER

Jupiter is the largest planet in our Solar System. It is a giant world of swirling clouds, powerful storms and colourful atmospheric bands. Its famous Great Red Spot is a gigantic storm that has lasted for centuries.

Planet Facts

Distance from Sun 778 Million km
Diameter 139,820 km
Day Length 9.9 Hours
Known Moons Many Moons
Year Length 11.86 Earth Years
Planet Type Gas Giant

Explore Jupiter

  • Great Red Spot: A huge, long-lasting storm in Jupiter's atmosphere.
  • Largest Planet: Jupiter is more than 11 times Earth's diameter.
  • Gas Giant: It is made mostly of hydrogen and helium.
  • Faint Rings: Jupiter has a thin ring system.
  • Galilean Moons: Io, Europa, Ganymede and Callisto are its four largest moons.
