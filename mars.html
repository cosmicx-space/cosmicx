Mars

The Red Planet

About Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and is often called the Red Planet because iron minerals in its soil have oxidized, giving the surface its reddish appearance.

Mars is a cold, rocky world with mountains, valleys, volcanoes, impact craters and polar ice caps. It has been one of the most extensively explored planets beyond Earth.

Surface & Landscape

Mars has one of the largest volcanoes known in the Solar System, Olympus Mons. It also contains Valles Marineris, a gigantic canyon system stretching thousands of kilometres across the surface.

Atmosphere

Mars has a very thin atmosphere composed mostly of carbon dioxide. Because the atmosphere is so thin, the planet cannot retain heat as efficiently as Earth.

Water on Mars

Evidence shows that liquid water existed on ancient Mars. Today, water is mainly found as ice beneath the surface and around the polar regions.

Exploring Mars

Mars has been explored by orbiters, landers and rovers. Robotic missions have studied its rocks, atmosphere, climate and geological history while searching for clues about its past.

Interesting Facts

  • Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun.
  • It has two small moons: Phobos and Deimos.
  • Olympus Mons is the largest known volcano in the Solar System.
  • Mars has polar ice caps.
  • A Martian day is called a sol.
  • Its reddish colour comes from iron-rich minerals.
