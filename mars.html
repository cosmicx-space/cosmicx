The Red Planet

MARS

Mars is a fascinating red planet with dusty deserts, enormous volcanoes, deep valleys and frozen polar caps. Scientists study Mars to understand its past and whether it may once have supported microbial life.

Planet Facts

Distance from Sun 228 Million km
Diameter 6,779 km
Day Length 24.6 Hours
Moons Phobos & Deimos
Year Length 687 Earth Days
Average Temperature -65°C

Explore Mars

  • Olympus Mons: The largest known volcano in the Solar System.
  • Valles Marineris: A vast canyon system stretching thousands of kilometres.
  • Red Surface: Iron minerals in the soil give Mars its rusty colour.
  • Two Moons: Mars has two small moons named Phobos and Deimos.
  • Exploration: Robotic rovers explore the Martian surface and study its geology.
