About Mercury

Mercury is the smallest planet in our Solar System and the closest planet to the Sun. Its ancient rocky surface is covered with craters. With almost no substantial atmosphere to retain heat, Mercury experiences extreme temperature differences between day and night.

☀️ Distance

About 57.9 million km from the Sun on average.

🌑 Planet Type

A small, rocky terrestrial planet.

⏱️ Orbital Period

88 Earth days to complete one orbit around the Sun.

🌡️ Temperature

Roughly −180°C at night to 430°C during the day.

🪨 Surface

A heavily cratered landscape, somewhat like Earth's Moon.

🌙 Moons

Mercury has no natural moons and no ring system.

Discover Mercury

01 · Extreme Temperatures

Mercury's thin exosphere cannot hold heat efficiently. Its sunlit surface gets extremely hot while its nightside becomes very cold.

02 · A Long Solar Day

Mercury rotates once in about 59 Earth days. Sunrise to sunrise on its surface takes about 176 Earth days.

03 · Ancient Craters

Impact craters preserve clues about the early Solar System. Caloris Basin is one of Mercury's most prominent impact features.

04 · Ice in Shadow

Water ice exists in permanently shadowed regions near Mercury's poles, where sunlight cannot reach the crater floors.
