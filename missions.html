Space Missions

Humanity's Journey Beyond Earth

🚀 Apollo 11

Apollo 11 was the first crewed mission to successfully land humans on the Moon. Astronauts Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin walked on the lunar surface while Michael Collins remained in lunar orbit.

1969 Moon NASA First Lunar Landing

🛰️ Voyager Missions

Voyager 1 and Voyager 2 were launched to explore the outer Solar System. They provided detailed observations of the giant planets and their moons and later continued into interstellar space.

1977 Outer Solar System Voyager 1 Voyager 2

🔭 Hubble Space Telescope

The Hubble Space Telescope has observed distant galaxies, nebulae, stars and other cosmic objects. Its observations have contributed greatly to our understanding of the universe.

1990 Earth Orbit Space Telescope

🔴 Mars Exploration

Robotic spacecraft and rovers have explored Mars to study its geology, atmosphere, climate and history. Missions such as Curiosity and Perseverance have investigated the planet's surface in detail.

Mars Rovers NASA Planetary Science

🌌 James Webb Space Telescope

The James Webb Space Telescope observes the universe primarily in infrared wavelengths. It studies distant galaxies, stars, planet-forming systems and the atmospheres of some exoplanets.

2021 Infrared Deep Space Space Telescope
