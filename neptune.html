The Blue Giant

NEPTUNE

Neptune is the eighth and farthest planet from the Sun. This distant ice giant is known for its deep-blue appearance, extremely powerful winds and dynamic atmosphere. It takes nearly 165 Earth years to complete one orbit around the Sun.

Planet Facts

Distance from Sun 4.5 Billion km
Diameter 49,244 km
Day Length About 16.1 Hours
Known Moons 16
Year Length 165 Earth Years
Planet Type Ice Giant

Explore Neptune

  • Extreme Winds: Neptune has some of the fastest winds measured on any planet.
  • Deep Blue World: Methane absorbs red light, contributing to its blue appearance.
  • Ice Giant: Its interior contains hot, dense materials including water, ammonia and methane.
  • Triton: Neptune's largest moon travels in a retrograde orbit.
  • Long Year: One Neptune year lasts about 165 Earth years.
