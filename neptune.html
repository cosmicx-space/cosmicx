Neptune

The Windy Blue World

About Neptune

Neptune is the eighth and most distant major planet from the Sun. It is an ice giant with a deep blue appearance and a dynamic atmosphere.

Neptune is extremely far from the Sun and takes many decades to complete a single orbit.

Powerful Winds

Neptune is known for having some of the fastest winds measured in the Solar System. Its atmosphere contains large storms and rapidly changing cloud formations.

Atmosphere

Neptune's atmosphere is primarily hydrogen and helium, with methane contributing to its blue colour. Beneath the atmosphere lies a deep layer rich in water, ammonia and other compounds.

Moons & Rings

Neptune has a collection of known moons, including Triton, its largest moon. Neptune also possesses a faint and dusty ring system.

Exploring Neptune

Voyager 2 remains the only spacecraft to have made a close flyby of Neptune. The mission revealed detailed information about its atmosphere, rings and moons.

Interesting Facts

  • Neptune is the eighth planet from the Sun.
  • It is an ice giant.
  • It has extremely powerful atmospheric winds.
  • Triton is Neptune's largest moon.
  • Neptune has faint rings.
  • One Neptune year lasts about 165 Earth years.
