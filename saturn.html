Saturn

The Ringed Giant

About Saturn

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second-largest planet in the Solar System. Like Jupiter, Saturn is a gas giant made primarily of hydrogen and helium.

The Famous Rings

Saturn is best known for its spectacular ring system. The rings are made mostly of countless pieces of ice and rocky material. They range in size from tiny particles to much larger fragments.

Atmosphere

Saturn has strong winds and bands of clouds in its atmosphere. Its upper atmosphere contains hydrogen, helium and small amounts of other gases.

Moons

Saturn has a large collection of moons. Titan is its largest moon and has a thick atmosphere, while Enceladus is known for icy material and water-rich plumes.

Exploring Saturn

The Cassini-Huygens mission provided detailed observations of Saturn, its rings and its moons, greatly expanding scientific knowledge of the Saturn system.

Interesting Facts

  • Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun.
  • It is famous for its extensive ring system.
  • Saturn is less dense than water.
  • Titan is Saturn's largest moon.
  • Saturn is a gas giant.
  • Its atmosphere contains powerful winds and storms.
