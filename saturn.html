The Ringed Planet

SATURN

Saturn is a magnificent gas giant famous for its spectacular rings made mainly of ice, rock and dust. Its pale golden atmosphere and mysterious moons make it one of the most fascinating worlds in our Solar System.

Planet Facts

Distance from Sun 1.43 Billion km
Diameter 116,460 km
Day Length About 10.7 Hours
Known Moons Many Moons
Year Length 29.4 Earth Years
Planet Type Gas Giant

Explore Saturn

  • Beautiful Rings: Saturn's rings contain countless pieces of ice and rocky material.
  • Low Density: Saturn is less dense than water on average.
  • Titan: Its largest moon has lakes and seas of liquid methane and ethane.
  • Enceladus: This icy moon shoots water-rich plumes into space.
  • Fast Rotation: Saturn completes a rotation in roughly 10.7 hours.
