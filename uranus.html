Uranus

The Tilted Ice Giant

About Uranus

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun and an ice giant. Its blue-green appearance comes mainly from methane in its upper atmosphere, which absorbs red light.

A Planet on Its Side

Uranus has an extreme axial tilt of about 98 degrees. Because of this unusual orientation, the planet experiences very dramatic seasonal changes during its long journey around the Sun.

Atmosphere

The atmosphere of Uranus is primarily hydrogen and helium with methane present in smaller quantities. The upper atmosphere can experience powerful winds and complex cloud activity.

Rings & Moons

Uranus has a system of narrow, dark rings and a collection of known moons. Several of its moons have unusual shapes and interesting geological features.

Exploring Uranus

Voyager 2 is the only spacecraft to have made a close flyby of Uranus. The mission provided important information about the planet's atmosphere, rings and moons.

Interesting Facts

  • Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun.
  • It is an ice giant.
  • Its axis is tilted about 98 degrees.
  • Methane contributes to its blue-green colour.
  • Uranus has a faint ring system.
  • Its seasons can last many Earth years.
