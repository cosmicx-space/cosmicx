The Ice Giant

URANUS

Uranus is a distant ice giant with a beautiful blue-green atmosphere. It rotates on its side, giving it one of the most unusual seasons in the Solar System. Its faint rings and extremely cold atmosphere make it a fascinating world.

Planet Facts

Distance from Sun 2.87 Billion km
Diameter 50,724 km
Day Length About 17.2 Hours
Known Moons 28
Year Length 84 Earth Years
Planet Type Ice Giant

Explore Uranus

  • Sideways Rotation: Uranus has an extreme axial tilt of about 98 degrees.
  • Blue-Green Colour: Methane in its atmosphere absorbs red light.
  • Faint Rings: Uranus has a system of narrow, dark rings.
  • Extreme Cold: Its atmosphere can reach exceptionally low temperatures.
  • Long Seasons: Each season lasts roughly 21 Earth years.
