About Venus

Venus is the second planet from the Sun. Its thick atmosphere is dominated by carbon dioxide and its clouds contain sulfuric acid. An intense greenhouse effect makes Venus the hottest planet in our Solar System. Its bright appearance comes from sunlight reflected by its dense cloud cover.

☀️ Distance

About 108.2 million km from the Sun on average.

🌫️ Atmosphere

Extremely thick atmosphere, mostly carbon dioxide.

⏱️ Year

225 Earth days to orbit the Sun.

🌡️ Temperature

Average surface temperature is about 465°C.

🔄 Rotation

Rotates slowly in the opposite direction to most planets.

🌙 Moons

Venus has no known natural moons or rings.

Discover Venus

01 · Extreme Heat

Venus is hotter than Mercury despite being farther from the Sun. Its dense atmosphere traps heat through a powerful greenhouse effect.

02 · Sulfuric Acid Clouds

Thick clouds of sulfuric acid surround Venus and reflect much of the sunlight that reaches its atmosphere.

03 · Backward Rotation

Venus rotates retrograde, so the Sun would appear to rise in the west and set in the east.

04 · Volcanic Landscape

Venus has extensive volcanic features, including large volcanoes and broad lava plains.
